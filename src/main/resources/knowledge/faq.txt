Q: What is your return policy?
A: We accept returns within 30 days of purchase with a valid receipt. Items must be unused and in original packaging to qualify for a full refund.

Q: Do you offer free shipping?
A: Free shipping is available on orders over $50. Orders below that threshold have a flat $4.99 shipping fee.

Q: How long do orders take to ship?
A: It takes 3-5 business days to allow time for processing. Expedited 2-day shipping is available at checkout for an additional fee.

Q: What are your in-store hours?
A: Our hours are 9AM - 5PM every day. Holiday hours may vary and will be posted on our website in advance.

Q: Can I purchase an item online and return it in person?
A: Yes, you can return an online item at a physical location as long as you have the email receipt. In-store staff can look up your order by email if you have lost the receipt.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay. We do not accept cash for online orders.

Q: Do you have a loyalty program?
A: Yes, our Nova Rewards program gives you 1 point per dollar spent. Points can be redeemed for store credit at a rate of 100 points per $5.
